# FWG: April 12, 2026

## Saints Becoming Saints: Only God Can Make Us Grow
1 Corinthians 3:5-9

## Discussion Questions:

1. Discuss Keller’s take on why ego is such a problem. Reminder: It leaves you empty, hurt, busy, and fragile. What is the profound difference in thinking less of yourself and thinking of yourself less? 
2. Discuss the 3rd “C” of our discipleship pathway: Chore. What do you like and not like about this aspect of our church’s life? Why is it so important to our health and faithfulness as a church?
3. Why is it important to starve the pride that exists within you? How does your time alone with Jesus, in His Word and through prayer, give you the resources you need to live this way?
4. Where can one look within themselves to find satisfaction in their obedience? How does unhealthy comparison and boasting threaten this satisfaction? 
5. How does Paul and Apollos exemplify healthy dependency within the church? How does this play out in the life of the church today (Hint: management and ministry)?
6. What is the difference between being rewarded for success and being rewarded for faithfulness? How is this text liberating?
