# FWG: April 12, 2026

## Saints Becoming Saints: Building our Church with Care
1 Corinthians 3:10-17

## Discussion Questions:

1. Much like the life-force that causes a plant to grow, the building of God’s church can only be attributed to the power of grace (3:10). Read Romans 1:16 as a group and talk about the power of the gospel in our church, in our homes, and in our daily life.
2. Discuss the foundation of a healthy church from what we learn in verses 10 and 11. Of what does that foundation consist and how do we insure that our foundation is strong?
3. Discuss what Paul means when he calls himself a skilled master builder in verse 10. How does this description of pastoral ministry help you to relate well to those who preach and teach God’s word to you here at First Baptist? How does this inform your prayer life as you intercede for your pastors? Take some time and pray for the ministerial staff of our church.
4. How do we ensure that the ministries of our church are of good quality? Consider the questions Pastor Jeff offered in the sermon: Does it make new disciples? Does it help the saints grow in their sainthood? Does it fit (hint: we must exegete the Bible, our church, and our community—where these matters intersect is where faithful ministry takes place)? Have we prayed for God to show us what to build? Will it last?
5. How does the day of the Lord motivate and inform believers who desire to be ready for Jesus’ return? Discuss the encouragement of reward and the warning of escaping through fire.
