# FWG: May 17, 2026

## Saints Becoming Saints: Cultivating a Healthy View of Self
1 Corinthians 3:18 - 4:5

## Discussion Questions:

1. In all honesty, can you offer an area of your life in which pure motives are hard to come by? Give some examples of good things that can be done with wrong motives.
2. Why does Paul consider pride and boasting a truly devastating sin? Why might a person deceive himself and not seek to align his life with God’s truthful assessment? How might boasting cause you to gaslight yourself?
3. How does the gospel redeem our thought life? Hint: In Jesus, God’s Word says that everything is yours. Go through the list of God’s gifts in verse 22 and talk about the one that is especially encouraging to you today.
4. What was the point of the quotation from Madonna? Why is this kind of thinking a broken cistern?
5. Describe Tim Keller’s observation of the ultimate verdict. What is it? Why does it turn our thought life upside down? What does it mean to say that the ultimate verdict leads to our performance, rather than the reverse, which would claim we perform to gain a favorable verdict? How do we see the gospel in this principle?
6. What do we learn from Paul about the opinions of others? What do we learn about the opinion we hold for ourselves? Why should both of these opinions be subjected to the ultimate verdict of the Lord?
7. Why is a life lived for the commendation of God alone so liberating?
