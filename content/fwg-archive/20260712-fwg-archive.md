# FWG: May 17, 2026

## Saints Becoming Saints: The Right Way to Make Things Right
1 Corinthians 6:1-11

## Discussion Questions:

1. Discuss the place of the church and the place of the government in handling disputes between believers. Note: 1 Corinthians involves civil disputes, while the government handles criminal offenses. Some key texts to read along with 1 Corinthians 6:1-11 are Romans 13:1-7 and 1 Peter 2:13-17.
2. How does true Christian love set the tone and tenor for a church to handle disputes?
3. How does our eschatology, the study of last things, inform our ecclesiology in this text?
4. Why should we have confidence that we can solve disputes when they arise within the church?
5. What does Paul mean by referencing a Christian’s experience of being washed, sanctified, and justified (verse 11)? How does this truth impact our thinking and behavior as we seek to settle our disputes?
6. Ken Sande o=ers the following helpful insights as to how we can apply the gospel to our disagreements. Take some time to discuss the ones that strike you the most:
    1. Remember the “Golden Result.”
    2. Clarify the problem to eliminate misunderstanding.
    3. Use the gospel and not the law to settle our disagreements.
    4. Seek to di=use the problem by using these helpful questions:
        1. How do you feel because of this conflict?
        2. What have you done to contribute to the problem?
        3. What do you think would please God as we work through this problem?
        4. What steps have already been taken to resolve this problem?
        5. What is the next right thing for us to do?
