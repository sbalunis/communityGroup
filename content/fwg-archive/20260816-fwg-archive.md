# FWG: August 16, 2026

## Saints Becoming Saints: An Appeal for Self-Control
1 Corinthians 7:36-40

## Discussion Questions:

1. Tim Keller encourages us to not listen to ourselves, but to talk to ourselves. How do we preach the gospel to ourselves as we strive to bring our desires under control?
2. Define self-control as a group. How does self-control help us make decisions that glorify God?
3. What does it mean to be filled with the Spirit? What are practical ways to pursue a fresh filling of the Spirit every day? Why is this important?
4. What advice would you give a couple thinking about getting married? How long would you advise them to be engaged?
5. Why is singleness described by Paul as being the better way?
6. In what ways should an engaged person treat their fiancé as if they are married (Hint: Eph 5:25-26)?
7. In counseling widows in their pursuit of marriage, how does contentment in Christ give the necessary foundation to honor God in a decision to marry or remain single?
8. James 1:17 promises that all good gifts are from above. Why does God, the good giver, gift us in ways we don’t always desire? How do we trust Him when we feel this is happening?
