# FWG: August 23, 2026

## Saints Becoming Saints: Free to Love Others Well
1 Corinthians 8

## Discussion Questions:

1. Our culture can make it challenging to stand for truth while loving others deeply at the same time. Discuss how Spirit-filled living allows a believer to bring these important life principles together.
2. Discuss the significance of meat sacrificed to idols in this text. Discuss the reasons why this issue was causing a separation between the “knowers” and the “weak.”
3. As you think of the principles of 1 Corinthians 8, how do they inform your decision to attend a wedding of a couple pursuing a marriage that does not align with biblical marriage.
4. How can a person protect themselves from arrogance while growing in their knowledge of God’s truth? How does knowledge deepen a believer’s love?
5. I have heard it stated by Christian artist Shai Linne that right orthodoxy (doctrine) leads to right doxology (worship). How does this truth, found in verses 4-6, help to create an environment in which a new believer can learn and thrive? How does a mature believer contribute to this culture within a church?
6. A conscience is the faculty within a person that helps them determine right from wrong. Why is it unwise to insist on expressing Christian freedom at the expense of a young believer’s conscience?
7. If causing a person to struggle is truly a sin, how is God calling you to apply verse 13: “If what I eat causes a brother or sister to fall into sin, I will never eat meat again, so that I will not cause them to fall” (NIV). I used the example of social drinking in the sermon. Feel free to discuss this issue or consider another application.
