# FWG: August 30, 2026

## Saints Becoming Saints: For the Sake of the Gospel
1 Corinthians 9:1-27

## Discussion Questions:

1. Take some time and talk about the role of pastor and deacon within our church. Pray, in particular, for our newly ordained deacons, Scott Allen, Saindon Balunis, Andrew Cooper, and Sean Murray. Ask the Lord to give them strength and fortitude as they serve our church in such an important time in our church’s history.
2. How do you bring together the joy found in the gospel (Psalm 16:11) and the suffer that discipleship often brings?
3. As you consider the workmanship that Paul pointed to in verse 2, consider who the workmanship connected to you might be. Who have you shared Christ with?
Who are you praying for in regard to the person’s salvation? Who are you intentionally discipling each week? Discuss how you might make it a regular pattern to invest in the workmanship that God has led for you to serve and share. What might be getting in the way?
4. Do any of the rights of which Paul does not partake surprise you? What does this teach us about the call to true discipleship?
5. If preaching the gospel is the disciple’s reward, what implications does that have on your life each day?
6. What does it mean to be all things to all people so that you might win some (v 22)? What does this text teach us about the priority of soul winning (sharing the gospel to bring those who are lost to a place that they are asked to trust in Christ for salvation)? Does this text make you want to be taught and equipped in how to share your faith?
7. Discuss the prayer of preparation as an application of the discipline required of an authentic disciple in verses 24-27. It comes from Josh Smith’s The Man for the Day: “Lord, do everything you need to do in me so that You can do everything you want to do through me.”
