## Sermon

{{< collapse summary="**Sermon Summary**">}}

This Palm Sunday sermon by Dr. Jeff Pennington focuses on the triumphal entry of Jesus into Jerusalem (Matthew 21:1-11), highlighting that salvation is found only in the humble King. Dr. Pennington explores three key themes:

* **The Paradox of Stewardship:** While God is independent and needs nothing, He allows us to participate in His work. Just as the owner of the donkey gave it to Jesus when asked, we have the privilege of contributing our resources to God's purpose.
* **The Practice of Obedience:** The disciples obeyed Jesus' specific instructions regarding the donkey without question. We are called to similar obedience, allowing God to use us like a sharp scalpel for His purposes.
* **The Promise of Parousia:** The prophecy in Zechariah 9:9 was fulfilled when Jesus entered Jerusalem. This points forward to His second coming—His final arrival—where He will return in glory, not humility.

## Discussion Questions

### Question 1

Talk about the attribute of God’s independence.
* How do we correctly understand the paradox that God who needs nothing equips the two disciples to say, if questioned, “The Lord needs them”?

### Question 2

* Discuss how a desire to be a good steward deepens our assurance of our salvation.

### Question 3

* Why is it true that obedience to the Lord is always costly?
* How does our willingness to obey the Lord, like the two disciples in verse 6, strengthen our assurance?

### Question 4

Discuss the prophecy found in Zechariah 9:9 in how it is fulfilled in Jesus’ first coming. Then discuss the prophecy of Zechariah 14:1-7 and will be fulfilled in Jesus’ second coming.

* How do these verses impact and inform your daily walk with the Lord?
