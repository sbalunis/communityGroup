## Discussion Questions

### Question 1

Friday, on Al Mohler’s Briefing, he discussed the problematic conclusion of renown a 20th century Jewish Rabbi who asserted that you can have Judaism without the Jewish religion. All you need to have an enduring Judaism is to have a Jewish people. Apply this thinking to the church in light of 1 Cor. 15.
* Why is it true that you cannot have Christianity if all you have are a Christian people?
* What does this chapter say in response to this heretical conclusion?

[1 Corinthians 15](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20Cor%2015&version=ESV)

### Question 2

Discuss the premise of the sermon, the Greek words translated “saw” show the different responses people have to the empty tomb (blepó, theóreó, and eidó).
* *Blepó (βλέπω):* a quick glance
* *Theóreó  (θεωρέω):* a long gaze
* *Eidó (εἴδω):* a look that contains understanding; this look leads a person to perceive the truth

### Question 3

Discuss Mary Magdelene’s response to the empty tomb.
* How would you share the gospel with someone who you discern is in a similar place?

### Question 4

Consider Peter’s response to the empty tomb.
* Why is it a frightening response?
* How would you lovingly challenge someone you discern is in this place?

### Question 5

Consider John’s response to the empty tomb and reflect on your own story:

* When did you first truly look at the truth claims of Jesus and believe?
* When were you born again?

If you feel comfortable, share your testimony with the group. There’s no pressure to answer—feel free to share only if and when you’re ready.
