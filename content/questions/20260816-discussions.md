## Discussion Questions

### Question 1

Pastor Jeff noted that instead of just listening to our own internal impulses, we should talk to ourselves. Drawing on the idea of preaching the gospel to our own hearts, how can we do this practically when we feel our desires—what he called the 'beast' of sinful passions—starting to go unchecked?

### Question 2

The sermon defined self-control as 'controlling the beast of your own sinful passions'. As a group, how does cultivating this discipline change the way we approach big life decisions, and why does Pastor Jeff emphasize that this is essential for our growth in sainthood?

### Question 3

Pastor Jeff emphasized that while we are baptized by the Spirit once, we need a fresh 'filling' of the Holy Spirit every single day to avoid living by our flesh. What does a daily, practical routine look like for you to ask God for this filling, and why is this the only way to truly keep our desires submitted to King Jesus?

### Question 4

Pastor Jeff expressed some concern about modern cultural trends regarding long engagements and waiting too long to marry. Given his perspective on prioritizing personal purity and obedience to Jesus over the 'pomp and circumstance' of a wedding, what kind of advice would you offer a couple today, and why did he suggest a shorter engagement?

### Question 5

In the passage, Paul discusses the benefits of singleness, which Pastor Jeff noted allows for a more 'unencumbered' way to serve God. Looking back at the sermon, why is this described as the 'better' way, and how can we better support those in our church who are currently in this season of life?

### Question 6

Referencing Ephesians 5:25-26, Pastor Jeff challenged us to 'treat your fiancé as if you are married'. What does it look like to act as a tool in God’s hand to help your future spouse grow in their holiness, rather than just seeking your own happiness in the relationship?

### Question 7

When discussing the transition of widowhood, Pastor Jeff highlighted the importance of finding one's foundation in Christ before deciding to remarry or remain single. How does being truly content in Jesus act as a protective barrier against the 'worldly anxieties' that can come when we feel pressured to change our circumstances?

### Question 8

Pastor Jeff reminded us that God is the giver of every good gift, even when He gives us gifts we might not have chosen for ourselves. When you face a season where God’s plan feels different from your desires, how do you practically turn back to Scripture—like Matthew 6:8 or James 1:17—to rebuild your trust in His goodness?
