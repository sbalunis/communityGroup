> ### I Am the True Vine
> ^1^ “I am the true vine, and my Father is the vinedresser. ^2^ Every branch in me that does not bear fruit he takes away, and every branch that does bear fruit he prunes, that it may bear more fruit. ^3^ Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you. ^4^ Abide in me, and I in you. ## Discussion Questions

*From Proving My Worth to Proven in Him*  
**Text:** John 15:1–20  
**Central Question:** What are you trying to prove?  
**Big Idea:** We don’t live FOR God’s approval. In Christ, we live FROM God’s love.

- **Where do people feel the most pressure to prove themselves?** 
- How does that pressure change as we grow older?

### Connect 
**As we stay CONNECTED to Christ, He changes us from the INSIDE OUT, and we bear FRUIT that lasts through every season.**

{{< collapse summary="**John 15:1-5**" >}}

> ^1^ “I am the true vine, and my Father is the vinedresser. ^2^ Every branch in me that does not bear fruit he takes away, and every branch that does bear fruit he prunes, that it may bear more fruit. ^3^ Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you. ^4^ Abide in me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit by itself, unless it abides in the vine, neither can you, unless you abide in me. ^5^ I am the vine; you are the branches. Whoever abides in me and I in him, he it is that bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing.

{{< /collapse >}}

- What area of life are you most tempted to rely on for your sense of significance? 
- **What might depending on Christ look like in an ordinary day? Describe a situation in which you tend to rely on yourself instead.**
- What does it look like to be connected to God in every day life? 

### Cultivate
**CULTIVATING a Christ-like Character requires CONSISTENCY. And as you remain in Him, look to Him to produce fruit through His Word and Spirit.**

{{< collapse summary="**John 15:6-11**" >}}

> ^6^ If anyone does not abide in me, he is thrown away like a branch and withers; and the branches are gathered, thrown into the fire, and burned. ^7^ If you abide in me, and my words abide in you, ask whatever you wish, and it will be done for you. ^8^ By this my Father is glorified, that you bear much fruit and so prove to be my disciples. ^9^ As the Father has loved me, so have I loved you. Abide in my love. ^10^ If you keep my commandments, you will abide in my love, just as I have kept my Father's commandments and abide in his love. ^11^ These things I have spoken to you, that my joy may be in you, and that your joy may be full.

{{< /collapse >}}

- **Where have you seen Christ gradually change your attitudes or actions? Where do you still need to grow?**
- Do you train spiritually every day? What are some things you would add to a spiritual training plan?

### Choose
**What we CHOOSE matters. CHOOSE to LOVE like Jesus.**

{{< collapse summary="**John 15:12-17**" >}}

> ^12^ “This is my commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you. ^13^ Greater love has no one than this, that someone lay down his life for his friends. ^14^ You are my friends if you do what I command you. ^15^ No longer do I call you servants,[a] for the servant does not know what his master is doing; but I have called you friends, for all that I have heard from my Father I have made known to you. ^16^ You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you that you should go and bear fruit and that your fruit should abide, so that whatever you ask the Father in my name, he may give it to you. ^17^ These things I command you, so that you will love one another.

{{< /collapse >}}

- How does Jesus’ command to love “as I have loved you” deepen our understanding of love?
- **What makes it difficult to love someone who disagrees with you, disappoints you, or offers nothing in return?**
- When has someone shown you the love of Christ through a costly or unnoticed act of service? What effect did it have on you?

### Commit
**COMMIT to Christ and be RESILIENT in times of HARDSHIP**

{{< collapse summary="**John 15:18-20**" >}}

> 18 “If the world hates you, know that it has hated me before it hated you. 19 If you were of the world, the world would love you as its own; but because you are not of the world, but I chose you out of the world, therefore the world hates you. 20 Remember the word that I said to you: ‘A servant is not greater than his master.’ If they persecuted me, they will also persecute you. If they kept my word, they will also keep yours.

{{< /collapse >}}

- Why did Jesus prepare His disciples for opposition? 
- **When hardship comes, what is your typical response?** 
- How can we be more like King Jehoshaphat in 2 Chronicles 20:12 - “We don’t know what to do, but we look to you!”…how can we love that out? 

### Conclusion
**We don’t live FOR God’s approval. In Christ, we live FROM God’s love.**

- **Which response needs your attention this week: depending on Christ, cultivating spiritual growth, loving someone sacrificially, or remaining faithful through difficulty?**
- What is one specific step you will take, and how can this group pray for you?
